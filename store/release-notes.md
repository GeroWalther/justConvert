## What's New

justConvert 2.0 is a complete rebuild.

• New: temperature, speed (including running pace), time, data, fuel economy, pressure and energy

• Decimal commas work, so "1,5" is read correctly
• Exchange rates load once and keep working offline
• More free units and currencies: GBP, JPY and CHF are now free alongside EUR and USD
• Save the conversions you use often and tap them to reuse
• Tap a result to copy it
• Dark mode, and a layout made for iPad
• Fixed: Restore Purchase now recognises an existing Pro purchase
• Fixed: very small results no longer round to zero
